description=Near-surface geophysics: GPR profiles in map context
about=Process GPR surveys in their map context. Import GSSI DZT files into grids placed from corners typed, picked on the map or read from a polygon, and fix, fit and destagger a line's placement afterwards, measuring the destagger from the data. Hover a line on the map to see its profile, with the profile cursor drawn back onto the map. Process each profile with steps you choose (time-zero, dewow, background removal, bandpass, gains, migration); nothing runs unless you add it. Pick reflections into a point layer, and build time and depth slices across a grid, shown on the map and exportable as a GeoTIFF. Cut a vertical section along any line you draw on a grid. The plugin is GPL-2.0-or-later; the bundled nsgeo core library is MIT.
version=0.4.0
changelog=0.4.0 - experimental
    - Cut: draw a line anywhere on a grid and see the vertical section beneath it, with picks; cuts move with their grid
    - Edit lines: fit lines to the grid's edges, destagger them, and Measure the destagger from the data
    - Time zero now sets the clock: the first break reads 0 ns, and picks, fits, cubes and GeoTIFFs record the zero they used
    - Slices: Whole site never refuses a fine cell, minimum curvature is 4-5x faster, cubes use less memory, and This grid and Whole site read a grid's lines alike
    - Grid velocity is optional (lines fall back to their headers); envelope and bandpass leaks fixed; Shift+scroll moves one Step per notch
    - Sites with cuts need 0.4.0 to open, and don't reopen a site in 0.3.0 after opening it in 0.4.0 (see the release notes)
    - Release notes: https://github.com/JamesZDonline/nsgeo/blob/v0.4.0/docs/releases/0.4.0.md
    0.3.0 - experimental
    - Edit lines: fix a line's label, offset, direction, start or grid after import, one line or several at once, and move lines to another grid
    - Picks follow their line when a grid or line is edited, and their depth follows the line's velocity
    - A grid that gets shorter or longer takes its reversed lines with the far edge
    - Migration (Stolt f-k): a new processing step that collapses diffraction hyperbolas, using the line's velocity
    - Slices: kriging, inverse distance or minimum curvature between lines, with a max distance and X/Y smoothing, replace Fill radius
    - Bandpass no longer wraps the direct wave into the bottom of the trace; grids start collapsed in the Survey panel
    - Release notes: https://github.com/JamesZDonline/nsgeo/blob/v0.3.0/docs/releases/0.3.0.md
    0.2.0 - experimental
    - Whole site: every grid's slice together on one map layer, with one depth slider and colour scale, and one GeoTIFF export
    - Slices remember the resolution you set for each grid and for the whole site; This grid's fill radius is set in line spacings
    - Velocity: measure a velocity by clicking a diffraction hyperbola; fits are saved with the survey and can be deleted
    - Profiles: reversed lines on a zigzag survey are mirrored onto one ruler per grid
    - Processing: a time cutoff dragged on the profile, a steadier gain curve, and a grouped Add step menu
    - Grid dialog: corners picked from the map or a layer in any CRS, checked with a fit
    - A Panels button, removing several lines at once, and fixes from the field
    - Release notes: https://github.com/JamesZDonline/nsgeo/blob/v0.2.0/docs/releases/0.2.0.md
    0.1.0 - first release, experimental
    - Sites and grids: import GSSI DZT files into grids placed from GNSS corners, digitised on the map, or read from a polygon
    - Map link: hover a line on the map to see its profile; the profile cursor and selected range are drawn on the map
    - Processing: time-zero, dewow, background removal, bandpass, gains and amplitude transforms, with a Difference view, presets and Apply to grid
    - Picking: picks from the profile into a point layer, with two-way time, distance, depth and velocity
    - Time and depth slices across a grid, shown on the map, saved as a cube or exported as a multi-band GeoTIFF
    - Known issues #22, #23 and #40 are on the issue tracker, each with a workaround in the getting-started guide: https://github.com/JamesZDonline/nsgeo/blob/v0.1.0/docs/guide/getting-started.md
