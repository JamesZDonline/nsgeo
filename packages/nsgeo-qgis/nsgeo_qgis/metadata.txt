[general]
name=nsgeo
qgisMinimumVersion=3.40
qgisMaximumVersion=4.99
supportsQt6=True
description=Near-surface geophysics: GPR profiles in map context
about=Process GPR surveys in their map context. Import GSSI DZT files into grids placed from GNSS corners, digitised on the map or read from a polygon. Hover a line on the map to see its profile, with the profile cursor drawn back onto the map. Process each profile with steps you choose (time-zero, dewow, background removal, bandpass, gains); nothing runs unless you add it. Pick reflections into a point layer, and build time and depth slices across a grid, shown on the map and exportable as a GeoTIFF. The plugin is GPL-2.0-or-later; the bundled nsgeo core library is MIT.
version=0.1.0
changelog=0.1.0 - first release, experimental
    - Sites and grids: import GSSI DZT files into grids placed from GNSS corners, digitised on the map, or read from a polygon
    - Map link: hover a line on the map to see its profile; the profile cursor and selected range are drawn on the map
    - Processing: time-zero, dewow, background removal, bandpass, gains and amplitude transforms, with a Difference view, presets and Apply to grid
    - Picking: picks from the profile into a point layer, with two-way time, distance, depth and velocity
    - Time and depth slices across a grid, shown on the map, saved as a cube or exported as a multi-band GeoTIFF
    - Known issues #22, #23 and #40 are on the issue tracker, each with a workaround in the getting-started guide
